# A Limited Infinity
Avery Lake, 2026

Self-contained, time-based HTML artwork. Silent. Continuous.

A finite alphabet unfolds through an endless sequence. Every finite combination of A–Z has a place, but the screen reveals only a fragment. Letters appear, pass, and remain available through the timeline.

A viewer recognises a name, remembers a place, or searches for a word. The sequence gives each letter the same duration. What it means depends on the life that meets it.

The possibilities have no final entry. Yet everything appears within the same boundaries. Can a language continue forever and still leave something unsaid?

## Companion
The companion shares the artwork’s exact sequence and beginning. Anyone can find a word’s designated arrival, save a calendar reminder where supported, and return to save or print that moment once it has arrived. Words are shared; there are no reservations or exclusive claims. A print is a finite excerpt, not an ownership certificate or a separately minted edition.
