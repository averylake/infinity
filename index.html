A finite alphabet. An endless sequence. +

Every finite combination of A–Z has a place in this sequence. The letters continue; meaning belongs to the encounter. A word can hold different lives. Once its moment arrives, anyone may save it.
Scroll upward to revisit. Return to the present to follow its unfolding.

The sequence concatenates A through Z, then AA through ZZ, then every three-letter combination, and onward. Spaces between combinations are omitted. Its mathematical rule has no final entry; this browser study has practical limits. The clock advances from one fixed beginning, including while the page is closed.
